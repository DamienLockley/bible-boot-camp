GITHUB PWA UPDATE
=================
Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to your existing bible-boot-camp
repository ROOT, preserving assets/. Replace old files and remove
obsolete images in assets/ after making a backup. Commit changes.
Upload the study-pdfs/ folder as well to enable Share Study PDF.
Open the app online to allow the new offline cache to install.
For reliable testing, close and reopen the installed app.

REFRESH BUTTON (NEW)
The small square refresh button next to Home checks for internet, updates the offline worker, clears its old cache, and reloads to fetch new studies. When offline, it keeps the existing cache intact. First publish your new files to GitHub.
