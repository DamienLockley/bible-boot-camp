Why I recommend reviewing these changes

  • Move early foundations forward: the historical Jesus and the Godhead currently appear at Studies 09 and 26, while the Spirit and sanctuary appear before the main Plan of Salvation study.
  • Keep connected teachings together: the Blue Stone, Law and Sabbath studies currently appear as 13, 06 and 14; this path places them together.
  • Put each prophecy in its learning sequence: the Messiah-focused Seventy Week Prophecy should introduce the timeline before the Judgment and 1844 studies, and before Antichrist, America and the Mark of the Beast.
  • Keep hope in order: explain the state of the dead before the Second Coming, then the Millennium and the end of evil.
  • Finish with lived faith: the new sequence concludes with the Remnant’s mission and then practical witnessing, while preserving freedom, gentleness and respect.

This is a proposed sequence, not an approved change. It should be checked against your full source set and your intended lesson flow before the review edition adopts it.
