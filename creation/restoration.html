Redemption

Can you still remember how God described his Creation works?

During Creation week, GOD described His Creation six times as ‘good’; but after looking at His completed creative works at the end of Day 6, HE described it as ‘very good’.

Genesis 1:31
And what does this mean again?

It means that God created everything flawless, perfect and beautiful.

Genesis 1:31
Can you remember what ‘freedom of choice’ means?

God created mankind with the ability to choose, because true love will always give you the freedom to choose. This means that you can choose for yourself whether you want to love, serve and obey God.

Deuteronomy 30:19; Joshua 24:15
What important point should we always remember when it comes to ‘freedom of choice’?

With this ability to choose comes a huge responsibility, because choices always have consequences.

Galatians 6:7-8
Did Adam & Eve make the right choice when God gave them His first instruction?

No!

Genesis 3:1-6
What was the consequences of their wrong decision?

When Adam and Eve chose to be disobedient to God, they also chose the tragic consequences that would affect them as well as all of their descendants, including you and me. Those consequences include things such as pests and diseases, famine, war, hatred, suffering, pain, sadness, evil and ultimately, death.

Genesis 3:16-19; Romans 5:12
Many people blame God and ask how He can be a loving God when we see all these terrible things happening in the world today. Is it God’s fault?

Certainly not — this is the sad and tragic consequences of disobedience to God

Romans 5:12; James 1:13-15
How is the reaction of people to God’s warning in the time of Noah similar today?

People in Noah’s time took absolutely no notice of the 120-year warning that God was about to bring a great destruction on the earth; and despite Noah’s continual warning while building the Ark, people ignored this serious warning and instead, they mocked and laughed at Noah every opportunity they got. That period was actually a period of grace because they had a 120-years to repent and trust in God. In this last period of grace of earth’s history, there is once again a very serious warning given in advance to everyone on earth; and that is that Jesus will soon return to Earth; and this time, HE is not only coming as the Savior of His children, but HE is also coming as the Judge of the universe to pass judgment on all the unbelievers and false Christians. [Read 2 Pet 3:10-12.] As it was in the days of Noah, there are people today who simply don’t believe that this warning is true at all, but this does not change the fact that it will happen.

Matthew 24:37-39; 2 Peter 3:3-10
What is sin?

Sin is anything we think, do, or say in violation of God’s commandments.

1 John 3:4
Where do we find those Commandments again?

In Exodus 20. God wrote these Commandments in stone with His own finger; and when something is written in stone, it means that it is forever. The Ten Commandments are like a mirror that shows us where we have been stained by sin.

Exodus 20:1-17; Exodus 31:18
Is all sin equally serious?

Sin is sin. In God’s eyes all sins are equally serious because all sins eventually lead to eternal death. Read Romans 6:23. God wants to give us eternal life, not eternal death.

Romans 6:23; James 2:10
Why does God have to punish sinners?

Because God is 100% love, but also 100% righteous and holy. Therefore, HE must punish the sinner because HE cannot be anything but a righteous God.

Romans 3:23-26; Romans 6:23
What is the punishment for sin?

Eternal death

Romans 6:23; Revelation 20:14-15
Who will save us?

Jesus stands ready and willing to forgive our sins and save us from eternal death.

John 3:16-17; Acts 4:12
How did Jesus become our Savior?

Even before the Creation, the heavenly Family already had a solution for this sad state of affairs. God is omniscient, which means, HE knows everything. HE created man with a free will and HE knew that they might choose to be disobedient; and if that were to happen, there had to be a plan to save mankind from sin and its deadly consequence. The plan was that ONE of the Persons of the Godhead would pay the penalty for our sin. That person was Jesus!

1 Peter 1:18-20; Revelation 13:8; Proverbs 8:25
Wasn’t it cruel of God the Father to allow His Son to pay the penalty that we deserve?

Read Joh 10:17-18 & Phil 2:6-8. In these verses we clearly see that Jesus, the Son Himself voluntarily offered to lay down His life because HE loves the Father and us infinitely! No-one forced Him to do so and the Son did not make any decision on His own, but always according to His Father’s will. The plan of salvation was therefore determined by the Godhead in unity.

John 10:17-18; Philippians 2:6-8
What is the Good News of the Gospel?

The Good News of the Gospel is that Jesus has already paid the penalty for our sins. HE became the perfect sacrifice by dying on the cross. But more importantly, HE rose from the grave to be the Savior of every person who chooses to accept His free gift of salvation.

1 Corinthians 15:3-4; John 3:16; Romans 5:8
What does it mean when I accept this free gift? Can I still continue doing the same

wrong things as before? Certainly not! By accepting this free gift, we choose to live our lives according to the example Jesus gave us. We cannot do that in our own strength, but only through the strength of Jesus, our Savior.

Romans 6:1-4; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Titus 2:11-12
What beautiful promise has God given to us if we made this choice?

Jesus promised that every person who made this choice would live with Him in the Heavenly Paradise - forever! Our Creator will recreate us physically by clothing us with immortality at His Second Coming. Never again will there be sin or death. Nobody will ever be afraid, sad or alone again. Read Romans 6:23

John 14:1-3; 1 Corinthians 15:51-54; Revelation 21:1-4
Could there perhaps be someone else or another way that could save us?

No! Read John 10:9 and 14:6

John 14:6; Acts 4:12
What can we do to be saved?

This is the most important question you will ever ask yourself. • We must confess our sin. (We should admit to God that we have sinned — ourselves) • We must be sincerely sorry and repent. ‘Repent’ means to turn away from sin. Turn to God. Tell Him how sorry you are for what you have done and stop doing it — turn away from it. • We must ask Jesus to cleanse us from our sin and to be our personal Savior. • We must honestly try not to sin again. If we apologize while already planning in our hearts to sin again, then we are not sincerely sorry and God cannot forgive us. Remember, God always looks at what is in our hearts (our minds). We cannot hide anything from Him, not even our thoughts. Therefore, when we confess and repent, we need to truly mean what we say. As hard as we try, we all seem to stumble and sin again every now and then. When this happens, happens, confess it to God immediately and ask for His forgiveness. Don’t wait until tonight when you go to bed. Don’t become discouraged! We simply cannot conquer sin in our own strength. We need Jesus every second of every day so that we, through His strength, can make the right choices. • Get to know God. Study His Word, the Bible. Pray and talk to God about everything in your life and as often as possible. We can do that in our thoughts or out loud. HE is interested in every little detail of our lives and HE wants us to share our thoughts, worries, hopes and dreams with Him. You can even write letters to Him if you like — as in a journal. We need to follow the instructions that He gave to us in His Word, The Bible, so that HE can protect us against THE enemy of mankind — satan. HE cannot do that if we choose to remove ourselves from His protective umbrella • And remember, even if you were the only person on earth, Jesus still would have laid down His life to save you. He loves us more than we can ever truly understand.

Acts 2:38; 1 John 1:9; Acts 3:19; Romans 10:9-10
