The Sabbath

How many days are in Creation Week?

Seven

Genesis 1:31; Genesis 2:1-3
Why should we also learn about the Seventh Day of Creation week?

Because the Seventh Day is inextricably linked to the Creation week. This means you cannot cut if off from the Creation Week,

Genesis 2:1-3
Do you think God was tired after creating all these awesome things?

No, because the Bible teaches that God never gets tired. Read this in Isa 40:28.

Isaiah 40:28
But why does the Bible teach that GOD ‘rested’ on the Seventh Day?

The Hebrew word used for ‘rest’ also means ‘to cease’, in other words to stop doing what you’ve been doing. God looked at everything He created and He was very pleased — it was complete, finished and perfect and together with Adam and Eve, HE delighted in it. So God was not tired, but rather, He set an example for us to follow.

Genesis 2:2-3; Exodus 20:11
Why did God create everything in six days?

God is almighty and of course He could have created everything over billions of years or even in an instant if He wanted to, yet, God chose to do so in six days so that we could see how He wanted us to organize our working days as opposed to the Sabbath day of rest.

Exodus 20:8-11; Genesis 2:2-3
Can we choose any random seventh day to keep the Sabbath?

No, God wants us to work for six days and rest on THE Seventh day, not a random seventh day of our own choosing. The word ‘the’ that is used before ‘Seventh day’ is called a definite article and this is always used to define something specific. The author is clearly referring to a specific day — the Seventh day. That is why our week is still exactly seven days long today.

Exodus 20:8-11; Leviticus 23:3
Is keeping the Sabbath important to God?

Indeed it is, which is why God made it part of his holy Ten Commandments which He wrote in stone with His Own Finger.

Exodus 20:8-11; Exodus 31:13-17
But why? Is the Sabbath Day not simply just another day?

There are two reasons why God commands us to keep the Sabbath Day holy. The first reason deals with the fact that God is our Creator and we can read about that in Exodus 20:8-11 where we find the Ten Commandments. Read that. The first reason that God gave for sanctifying (keeping holy) the Sabbath Day, is to acknowledge God as our Creator by reflecting (thinking) on His wondrous creative works, especially on the Sabbath. In Deut 5:12-15, however, God gives us another reason why we should keep the Sabbath holy and this deals with the fact that God is our Savior. Read that. In other words, when we choose to live in obedience to God’s Commandments - which includes keeping and honoring God Seventh Day Sabbath, then the Sabbath becomes a sign between us and God to show that we acknowledge Him as our Creator and our Redeemer (our Savior) and that we bow before HIS authority.

Exodus 20:8-11; Deuteronomy 5:12-15; Ezekiel 20:12, 20
Is the Sabbath Command not only for the Jews?

No. Israel (also known as the Jews) is commonly known as ‘God’s people’, but the Bible tells us that not only are the Jews God’s people, but also every person who chooses to believe in Him. That includes you and me! God makes no distinction between Jews and Gentiles, but He certainly distinguishes between believers and unbelievers. Read in Gal 3:26-29 how the Apostle Paul explains it. Remember that the Sabbath Day was instituted at Creation, before any nation such as the Jews even existed. It is therefore a command for all of mankind, not only the Jews.

Mark 2:27; Isaiah 56:6-7; Galatians 3:26-29
Does God provide us with a beautiful promise regarding keeping the Sabbath holy?

Yes, read about that in Isa 58:13-14

Isaiah 58:13-14
What does it mean when we read that the Ten Commandments were written in stone?

It means that it is forever

Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 10:4
Are we going to keep the Sabbath in heaven, in other words, forever?

Indeed, read about that in Isa 66:22-23

Isaiah 66:22-23
Which day is the seventh day?

The Seventh Day Biblical Sabbath always has been and still is on a Saturday, not a Sunday

Exodus 20:8-11; Luke 23:54-56
Why do so many people believe it is on a Sunday?

Many people believe that the Seventh Day Sabbath was changed to the first day, or Sunday, because Jesus’ resurrection took place on the first day.

Mark 16:1-2; Luke 24:1
Has the Sabbath Day ever been changed?

No, the Sabbath Day has never been changed by God and there is absolutely NO verse in the Bible that commands us to keep Sunday which is the first day, holy.

Exodus 20:8-11; Matthew 5:17-19
Who sets the best example on this for us to follow?

Jesus, since He and His disciples kept the Sabbath en Jesus says to us: ‘If you love me, keep my commandments’ (John 14:15). In fact, Jesus obeyed this commandment even in His death by resting in the grave on the Sabbath Day.

Luke 4:16; Luke 23:54-56; John 14:15
Why do most Christian churches today honor Sunday (1st day) instead of Saturday?

They honor Sunday, instead of Saturday, because of a tradition that was instituted by the Roman Catholic Church - a long time ago, although many Christians don’t even realize this. The first Sunday Law, commanding people to keep holy the 1st Day (or Sunday) instead of the Sabbath was instituted in the year 321AD by Constantine. That is 1700 years ago! And to this day, most churches keep Sunday holy based on Roman Catholic Church tradition.

What did Jesus say about his unchanging Commandments, about tradition and about the breaking of His Commandments?

Read Matt 5:18-19 and Matt:15:9

Matthew 5:18-19; Matthew 15:9
Who was the cleverest person ever on earth?

King Solomon

1 Kings 3:12; 1 Kings 4:29-34
What did he have to say regarding the keeping of God’s Ten Commandments?

Ecc 12:13 (NIV) ‘Now all has been heard; here is the conclusion of the matter: Fear God and keep his commandments, for this is the duty of all mankind.’ How much of mankind? All of mankind!

Ecclesiastes 12:13
Is it really all about a day?

No, it is definitely not about a day, it is about authority. Do we bow before God’s authority (because we love Him), or do we bow before the authority of mankind and his traditions?

John 14:15; Matthew 15:9
Can we be saved by simply keeping the Ten Commandments?

No, we should keep the Commandments of God BECAUSE we are saved, not TO BE saved. Very important, only Jesus can save us and not our works — not even works of obedience, or simply keeping the Sabbath, can do that.

Ephesians 2:8-10; Titus 3:5; John 14:15
