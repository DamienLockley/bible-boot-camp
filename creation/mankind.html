Mankind

‘Then GOD said, Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness’. Who is the ‘Us’ that GOD is referring to?

These are the three Persons of the divine Godhead - our Heavenly Father, the Son of God (also called the ‘Word’ in John 1:1) and the Holy Spirit.

What did GOD mean when He said, ‘in Our image, according to Our likeness?'

Mankind is very special to GOD and even though GOD cares deeply about all of His creation, mankind is completely different from all animals, mainly because mankind was created in the image of GOD and therefore reflects something of GOD’s nature. So when looking at one another, we should see something of GOD, we should not see a person acting like an ‘animal’. This is why only humans (and not animals) are able to have a personal relationship with our Creator-GOD

Many people believe that we have an immortal soul, but what do we learn about this from Genesis 2:4 ‘And the LORD GOD formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.’?

We learn how God took the dust of the earth to form man and only after He breathed into his nostrils the breath of life (His Ruach or His Spirit),' did the ‘form’ become a living human being or a living soul. It does not say that God gave mankind a living soul, as though the soul was something separate, but God took the dust (the flesh) and breathed His breath (Ruach/Spirit) into it and with the merging of the two, man became a living soul.

What does this mean?

It means that the soul is not something separate. We ARE a living, mortal soul and we are only alive because GOD’s breath of life keeps us alive day after day.

What happens when we die?

Life is given by God alone, and when that life ends, the breath of life returns to God (Who gave it); your body becomes dust and your soul (your life) ceases to exist (Ecc 12:6-7).

How does the Bible describe death?

The Bible describes death as a ‘sleep’.

Do you know when you are asleep?

No, You only realize that you were asleep once you wake up; and that is exactly how it works when you die.

Will you know that you are dead when you die one day?

No, in the same way it is when you sleep, it is also with death. The day when you die, you will not know that you are dead and you also won’t know how long you’ve been dead. You will only realize that you were dead when you open your eyes again and see Jesus in front of you with a huge smile on His loving face.

Why do people, plants and animals die today?

We were never supposed to die, nothing was supposed to die, but because of sin (The Fall) — which we’ll earn about in the next episode, death came into Creation.

Would Adam and Eve have died if they didn’t sin?

If they remained faithful to God and if they didn’t sin, they would not have died, because death is the result of sin. God’s breath of life would have kept them alive if they didn’t sin.

Is there a solution to this sad problem of death?

Yes! Thankfully, Jesus has the solution because He gave us a free gift: A sacrifice which ensures that every person who chooses to believe in Him and does what He asks, will be resurrected at His second coming to the earth. And as soon as Jesus wakes us up, He will give us all a brand new immortal body. This means that we won’t get hurt, sick or even die, ever again. It further means that everyone who believes in Jesus and obey His Commandments, will live with Him in the Heavenly Paradise - forever! Never again will there be sin or death and you will never be afraid, sad or alone, ever again. Isn’t that something to really look forward to?

Do Christians need to fear death?

Even though the thought of death is quite scary, always remember that as far as God’s children are concerned, death is nothing but a dreamless sleep while awaiting our Heavenly Paradise.

What important task did God give to Adam on his first day of existence?

He had to give names to all the birds and land animals.

How could Adam have done all of that in just one day, especially when we look at the enormous variety of animals on earth today?

The Bible tells us that Adam did not have to search for the animals because God brought them to him. Of the livestock such as sheep, cattle, pigs, rabbits, horses and so on, there were probably less than twelve original created kinds. The ‘beasts of the field’ probably lived in the open plains. God did not tell Adam to give names to all the land animals on earth such as creeping God and insects for example. Only 2% of all creatures have a back-bone and most of these are actually fish. The Bible uses the word ‘kind’ and not the word ‘species’. Today there are many types of ‘dogs’ which include wolves, jackal, foxes, etc. In the beginning, however, there was only one dog/wolf-kind from which all the various wolves, foxes, jackal and dogs originated. So Adam certainly did not need to specifically name a Miniature Pincher or a Dachshund as such, because they were not yet in existence. He only named this original animal: Wolf or dog.

How long would it have taken?

At most, Adam had to name only about one thousand animals and bird kinds. If he named one kind every few seconds, it would have taken him only about three to four hours to complete the task.

How did God create Eve?

God caused Adam to fall into a deep sleep and during the very first operation ever, HE used one of Adam’s ribs to form his mate.

What does ‘Eve’ mean and why is this important?

Eve, means ‘life’, because Eve would eventually become the mother of all mankind. This means that every single person who ever existed, is related to Adam and Eve. In other words, every human being on earth is part of one and the same family!

Did Adam and Eve’s children have one less rib because of this?

No, the rib bone is the one bone that can regrow if the surrounding membrane, the periosteum, is not damaged. So Adam’s rib bone grew again and all their children were born with the right number of ribs.

We read in Genesis that God established marriage on Day 6. What exactly does the Bible say about this?

The union of marriage is between one man and one woman. God did not institute marriage to be between one man with many women, or one woman with many men, or between two women or between two men. The marriage between one man and one woman is therefore a Divine and holy institution. Anything outside of this is NOT recognized as the union of marriage by God, no matter how many human laws are made about it.

God also appointed Adam and Eve as custodians of His creation. What does that mean?

It means that they (and all of mankind) are supposed to care for the earth and look after it.

What does ‘Eden’ mean?

Pleasure / Joy

What did humans and animals eat in the beginning?

Plants (fruit, seeds, nuts)

What was the ONE thing that God told them they’re not allowed to do?

GOD explained to Adam and Eve that they could eat the fruit from all the trees in the beautiful garden, except the fruit of the ‘Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil’.

Was there enough food for them and all the animals?

Oh yes they had more than enough to eat because each fruittree, for example, produced more of its own kind through the seeds that were already in the fruit. The fruit would have always been fresh, sweet and nutritious.

How did God describe his Creation works?

During Creation week, GOD described His Creation six times as ‘good’; but after looking at His completed creative works at the end of Day 6, HE described it as ‘very good’.

What does that really mean?

It means that everything was created to be perfect and beautiful. The fruit did not rot and the leaves did not fall from the trees. There was no evil, suffering, pain, disease, or sadness and certainly no death. It was perfect.

How does the idea of evolution differ from that which is written in the Bible?

The Bible teaches how God created each of the animals (and humans) according to their kind, just as He did with the plants. GOD blessed the animals and told them to be fruitful and to multiply. This means that each kind would only produce more of its own kind. This is exactly how it still works today. Different kinds cannot mate and reproduce offspring together, in other words, one kind have never been able to, nor will it ever be able to, produce another kind as taught in evolution. Apes and humans are different kinds; Fish and frogs are different kinds, Birds and reptiles are different kinds; Dogs and cats are different kinds. Even though we have a large variety of dogs and cats today, dogs are still dogs, cats are still cats. There is absolutely no evidence in support of one kind evolving into another kind.

What are transitional forms?

Transitional forms are something that gradually changes into something else. If it were really true that one kind could change or evolve into another kind, we should be able to see millions upon millions of examples of transitional or in-between forms in the fossil record, which is the earth’s cemetery of the Flood. In-between forms are for example fish gradually changing into frogs, reptiles gradually changing into birds and apes gradually changing into humans. So these are the in-between forms that should exist if evolution were true, but there are NONE!
