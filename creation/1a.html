Creation Week · Topic 1a

Days 1 to 4

The beginning of creation

If God made everything, then who made God?

Everything that has a beginning, must have been made by someone. In other words, it needs a cause. The creation had a beginning, but God has no beginning and no end, because He is the eternal God.This means that He exists from eternity to eternity and that is why no one could have made God.

What four important things did God make on Day 1?

Time, space, matter and energy

How long before Christ came to earth, did creation occur, more or less?

About 4,000 years before Christ

How many years have passed since Christ was on earth?

In 2022 it is 2,022 years. In 2023 is will be 2,023 years and so on…

So how long ago (approximately) did God create everything?

About 6,000 years ago

How did God create everything?

In Psalm 33:9 it is written everything and: ’For He spoke, and it was done; He commanded, and it stood fast…’ God only has to speak, and everything happens exactly as He said.

Do you think there is a person or even a computer that could make something from nothing?

Definitely not

Do you think that planet earth looked the same on Day 1 as it does today? Why?

Probably not since the Bible describes the initial earth-body as a huge, fishapeless, watery lump ‘without form and void’ located well within ‘the deep’ waters… No one knows exactly what it looked like, only God

What is gravity?

Gravity is the force that keeps and everything around us ‘stuck’ to the earth

What would have happened if there was no gravity?

We (and everything around us) would have just oated around aimlessly

What was the ‘deep waters’?

The ‘deep waters’ was probably a giant ball (heavenly sphere) of liquid water which contained the initial earth.

What light did God create on Day 1? Was it the sun’s light?

No, the sun was only created on Day 4. The Bible explains that GOD is the Source of light, which means that all light comes from GOD.

How long does it take for the earth to rotate once?

One complete night and one complete day = 24 hours

When does the new day begin, according to the Bible?

At sunset, the new day begins

In Genesis 1:2 it is written: ‘and darkness on the face of the deep’. What was this darkness?

It was the evening part of Day 1 since the day begins with an evening (‘darkness’)

What did God create on Day 2?

A firmament

What is that?

God called it ‘heaven’. It is the blue air or atmosphere (blue sky) which we can see around us, as well as outer space around the earth where the stars are.

The Bible mentions three heavens. What is that?

The first heaven is the atmosphere (blue skies) where the birds fly (Genesis 1:20). The second heaven is outer space where planets and stars reside (Psalm 8) The third heaven is where GOD resides (Psalm 11:4 & 2 Corinthians 12:2)

Why do we need an atmosphere The atmosphere contains all the important gases that we and other organisms need to survive (O2, Co2, N)

What did God create on Day 3?

Early on Day 3, our solid, spherical planet Earth was formed (shaped/given form)out of the ‘waters below’; so on Day three, God created the dry land mass and the plants. This land mass probably settled and separated from the water mass, (similar to when sediments in water settle) and then pushed up out of the water.

Were the plants mature/fully grown, or was it seed?

Each kind of plant was fully grown, in other words, it was mature and already contained its own speci c seed within the plant so that it could bring forth more of its own kind. That is exactly how it still works today.

What did God create on Day 4?

The sun, moon and stars

From what did God possibly create the luminaries (lights)?

Possibly from ‘the waters above’

What about all the other planets and meteors, when were they created?

The Hebrew word for ‘stars’ is broader than the modern meaning and includes meteors (shooting stars) and planets (formerly called ‘wandering stars’)

Does God know how many stars there are?

Yes, He knows exactly how many there are; but do we? - NO! Psalm 147:4 ‘HE counts the number of the stars;’

Did God give names to all the stars?

Yes, He did. The Bibles says: Psalm 147:4 ‘He calls them all by name.’

What does that tell us?

That God is all-knowing (omniscient). He knows absolutely everything — even the number of hairs on your little head!

Why did God create the heavens and heavenly bodies ’luminaries’ (sun, moon, stars)?

To provide light for us on earth (the moon and the stars flat night and the sun during the day). They would also function as signs to measure the days, months and years. God has also given us the ability to behold and appreciate the beautiful sky to bring glory and honor to God, our Creator. In Psalm 19:1 it is written: ‘The heavens declare the glory of God; And the firmament shows His handiwork.’ Why don’t you read that entire beautiful Psalm?

Is our planet located in a special and unique position? i

Yes… (Why do you say so?) Exact distance from the sun; Earth’s speed of rotation is exactly right; Special position of Jupiter to protect earth against comets and meteors, etc.

What would’ve happened if the earth was just 5% further away from the sun All the water on earth would have frozen.

What would’ve happened if the earth was only 5% closer to the sun?

All the water would have boiled, vaporized and disappeared.

How do tides form?

Because of the gravitational pull of the moon and the sun on earth.

What do the tides do?

They help to keep the oceans and beaches nice and clean. It ‘stirs’ the water, thereby putting oxygen into it so that sea creatures can fibreathe. So the tides prevent the sea from changing into a smelly, green mass of stagnant water, without oxygen, which would cause all the sea creatures to die.

Extra question to think about. WHO is the Creator?

Jesus. Read John 1 and particularly verse 14 which clearly identi es Jesus as the Creator. (Many people believe it was God the Father.)
