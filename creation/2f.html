After the Flood — Life & Languages

What did the animals possibly eat in the time after they disembarked from the Ark?

The large amounts of vegetation, including the remnants of trees, which must have been stranded en masse after the Flood would have made the growth of many types of fungi (mushrooms) possible; Seaweed; Carrion; Carcasses; Fish trapped in pools; Insect, earthworm and rodent populations would have grown explosively. Various plants already began to grow before the ark inhabitants disembarked.

How did the plants grow again after the Flood?

Through vegetative propagation (where new plants grow from roots, stems or leaves, and where the normal reproductive organs such as seeds/flowers etc, are not involved). Also, seeds that are in a dormant or resting state can survive as ‘passengers’ for long periods, even when they are soaked in water (they could have survived on floating vegetation rafts). Many seeds were buried in early Flood deposits and later exposed by erosion. In addition, Noah may have taken various kinds of seed onboard as well.

How do we know that there were already some plants when they disembarked the Ark?

A dove brought an olive leaf to Noah in the Ark, which clearly means that olive trees and possibly a large variety of other plants had already begun to grow even before they ventured out of the Ark

How did the dinosaurs become extinct?

All the dinosaurs, except those in the Ark, were destroyed and buried during the Flood. One pair of every kind of land animal, including dinosaurs as well as birds and pterosaurs survived in Noah’s Ark, filling the earth again after the Flood. Since that time, many different animals have become extinct, not only the dinosaurs, as a constant reminder of the serious consequences of sin on Creation. After the Flood, animals and people began to fear each other, which is in harmony with Genesis 9:3 where humans were given permission to eat meat. This is why they were hunted to the point of possible extinction. As in the case of the Dodo of Mauritius, it is more than possible that dinosaurs became extinct due to human impact; especially since people saw them as such a threat.

Read Gen 9:18-19. Where do all the people of today come from according to this verse?

The whole earth was populated from the three sons of Noah - Sem, Ham and Japheth (and their wives) and their descent can clearly be traced back to Adam and Eve since Adam was Noah’s great, great, great, great, great, great, great (x7) grandpa.

How do we know that the earth is not over populated?

Because calculations have shown that a world population of six billion people could easily fit into a small country like England; and then each one would have about 20 square meters of space around him/her. The current (2022) world population of around 8 billions is not much more than that.

What is exponential growth?

This is when a populations’ growth doubles after every subsequent population. With regard to the human population, it means that after each subsequent generation, there were more people than during the previous generation.

What growth rate do we need to account for the number of people on earth today if it began with only eight?

Only 0.45% (instead of more than 1% today)

If we use the 0.45% population growth rate, how many times would the subsequent populations, beginning with only eight people, need to double to reach the current number of people on earth?

Only 30 times

Are there different human races?

No, there is only one human race, but their are different people (ethnic) groups)

Read Acts 17:26-27. Are we all related to Adam and Eve?

Yes, Eve means ‘life’ which makes sense because Eve is the mother of all humankind. From this verse we clearly learn that every human being on earth is related to the other because it is written: “And He has made from one blood every nation of men to dwell on all the face of the earth”.

Why do skin color differ?

We all have the same skin coloring pigment responsible for our skin color, called melanin and melanin works like a kind of sunblock. The more melanin you have, the darker your skin would be and the longer it would take for you to burn in the sun. The less you have, the lighter your skin color would be and the easier or faster you would burn in the sun.

Where do all the different languages come from?

It began at the Tower of Babel when God confused their languages because of their disobedience when He told them to distribute over the earth and fill it.

How did people distribute over the earth?

After God confused their languages (because of their disobedience), they could not understand one another any more. The people that were able to understand one another (because they now spoke the same specific language) began to group together, and eventually moved away to places where they could live and work together without so much confusion. And so God ensured that they distributed over the earth to fill it, anyway.

Why do people in Africa mostly have a dark skin color, while people in Europe mostly have a light skin color?

The people groups that understood one another distributed in all directions: Asia, North and South America, Europe, Africa and Australia. Some individuals in these groups would have had the same skin color, but there must have also been a wide variety of skin colors in each group. Groups that reached the warmer parts of Africa and Australia: Individuals with a very light skin color would have burned faster in the sun than those with darker skin, since they had less melanin to protect them from the sun. They may have contracted skin cancer, later becoming too ill to have children and eventually dying after being continually exposed to the sun. In time, mostly people with darker skin would therefore have survived in Africa and Australia since they were better adapted to the harsh climatic conditions in these areas as they had more melanin in their skin. Groups that reached the cooler, cloudier northern parts of Europe: All people need some sunshine on their skin so that they can produce Vitamin D. People with lighter skin only need a little bit of sun to do so, but people with darker skin need more sunlight since their darker skin would prevent them from absorbing much sunlight. Remember, melanin acts like a sunblock. Consequently, people with darker skins would have become very ill in these cloudy areas, because their bodies would not have been able to produce enough Vitamin D. As time went by, the darker-skinned people would have died while people with lighter skin would mostly have been able to survive in these areas with high cloud cover since they were better adapted to those climatic conditions.

The Inuit (Eskimo’s) have brown skin, yet they live where there is not much sun. Why don’t they suffer from Vitamin D deficiency?

They probably have the same genetic make-up for a medium-colour skin. Yet they don’t suffer from Vitamin D deficiency since they obtain most of their Vitamin D from their diet of predominantly fish.

Why did the confusion of languages at Babel become a stumbling block in the spreading of the Gospel?

Since people could not understand different languages, the Good News of the Gospel could not be spread to people who spoke other languages

How did God solve this problem?

God blessed specific people in the Christian Church with the gift of speaking in tongues — without them even having to learn the specific language (See Acts 2, 10 and 19). Speaking in tongues means to have the gift to speak in a specific understandable language so that the Gospel truth can be clearly understood by all people, nations and tongues (in other words, people from differing languages). The purpose is therefore to spread the Good News of the Gospel to all people and all languages.
