Destruction · Topic 2a

The Fall

Why are there rules?

Rules are mostly good for you and there to protect you — to keep you safe from harm.

What does the Bible teach regarding obedience to our parents?

The Bible teaches us that we should honor and obey our parents. This means that you should be respectful when talking to or about your parents and furthermore, you should obey their rules.

How do you think your parents feel when you are disobedient?

Because your parents love you, it makes them very sad when you are disobedient and they have to punish you. No good parent enjoys punishing a child.

Why is it important for you to be punished when you disobey your parents’ rules?

God has appointed parents to teach their children about obedience — to our parents and more importantly, to obey God. This is a parent’s most important duty: To raise godly children on behalf of God, our Heavenly Dad and to teach children about His love and grace. By punishing you for disobedience, teaches you that disobedience always has rather unpleasant consequences so that next time, you’ll hopefully think twice before doing what you know you shouldn’t do.

What does it mean when God says He gave us ‘freedom of choice’?

GOD created us with the freedom of choice, because true love must always give you the freedom to choose for yourself. This means that we can choose whether we want to serve, love and obey God, or not. BUT REMEMBER, choices always have consequences.

What is sin?

Sin is anything we think, do, or say in violation of GOD’s Commands as clearly outlined in the Ten Commandments given to us in Exodus 20. This is God’s Law and this Law shows us what sin really is. Read 1 John 3:4 and Rom 7:7-8.

Can this Law cleanse us from our sin?

So in reality, the Ten Commandments are like a mirror in which we can see where we became filthy because of sin. Water cannot wash away the stains caused by our sins, neither can simply staring into the mirror accomplish this. Only the blood of Jesus Christ, our Savior can cleanse us of our sins.

Why did God give us the Ten Commandments?

As we’ve learnt in the very first question about rules, God gave us the Ten Commandments because He loves us very much and He wants to protect us from the negative and harmful consequences of disobedience.

What about people that have never heard of the Commandments before?

Deep inside our hearts and minds, we all know very well what is right and what is wrong, because we were created in the image of God and because the Holy Spirit constantly reminds us of this. It remains our choice whether we will listen to that Voice, or not.

What was the first rule that God gave to Adam and Eve?

God told them not to eat from the ‘Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil’.

What would happen if they did eat the fruit from this tree?

Because Adam and Eve were still 100% obedient to God at that point, there was no sin in the world and they knew absolutely nothing about evil. But if they would eat from the fruit of this tree, they would find out what evil and sin really was.

What was God’s warning to Adam and Eve about the consequences of this?

God warned them and gave them a very good reason why they should not eat the fruit from that tree, because if they did, they would surely die! God wanted to protect Adam and Eve from harm by teaching them to be obedient to Him. He did not want them to become acquainted with sorrow and evil, but the choice was in their hands.

Was the snake that tempted Eve just a snake?

No, satan used the serpent as an instrument to talk to Eve. The devil or satan likes to use various instruments to stimulate our curiosity and to tempt us and eventually, to deceive us into disobeying God.

What was satan’s plan?

Satan’s objective was to get Eve to doubt God’s trustworthiness by making her doubt what God had said. He also cunningly twisted what God actually commanded.

Who told the first lie in the Bible and what was that lie?

Satan told the first lie, which is why Jesus called him the father of lies (John 8:44). This lie had to do with the mortality of mankind. Satan actually told Eve that God lied to them and that they would not really die. To this very day, people still believe this lie — that when we die, we simply ‘live on’ and immediately go either to heaven, or to hell because they believe mankind has an immortal soul. But the Bible teaches very clearly that man is a mortal soul. He does not have a soul as though it is something separate; and that mankind is mortal. Only God is immortal (1Tim 3:13 and 16). Satan also lied about something else: When he said that Adam and Eve would become ‘like God’. He implied that they would become as powerful as God.

What is the ‘unforgivable sin’ spoken of in the Bible?

The longer we continue to do the wrong thing, the less it will bother us the next time because in this way, we become insensitive to sin; and if it gets to a point that it doesn’t bother us any more when we sin (that we don’t feel guilty any more) then we won’t even want to ask for God’s forgiveness. God cannot forgive us unless we listen to the conviction of the Holy Spirit, confess our sin and ask for His forgiveness. We must always remain sensitive to sin and never become comfortable with it! It must always bother us when we do something wrong because the price Jesus paid for our sins, was terribly high. There is no sin that God is unwilling to forgive the truly repentant sinner

How did Adam and Eve feel after they sinned against God?

Adam and Eve immediately realized that they had done something wrong. They now had knowledge of sin and evil because they had been disobedient to God. Evil had now become a part of their lives. It changed them and it damaged their relationship with God. The character of innocence and righteousness they had, were tarnished and they realized that they were naked. They deeply regretted what they had done. They felt guilty and embarrassed about their disobedience. They now realized that it was not God who had lied to them, but satan, because they certainly did not feel so great after eating the fruit.

How do you feel after doing something wrong?

Let the children explain themselves and compare to how Adam and Eve felt (regret, sadness, shame…)

Adam and Eve tried to hide from God after they sinned. In which way are humans still doing this today

Just like Adam and Eve, we also try to hide behind ‘fig leaves’. We do it by either lying about it, or by trying to justify it, or by saying: “Oh well, it’s not that serious.” or ‘Everyone does it”

Why is it so dangerous to try and justify your wrongdoing (sin)?

Because sin is sin and God says that all sin is equally serious because all sin eventually leads to eternal death. God wants to give us eternal life, not eternal death.

What was Adam’s reaction when God asked him whether he ate fruit the tree?

Instead of Adam admitting to his own guilt, he immediately first blamed GOD and then Eve.

What was Eve’s excuse?

She blamed the serpent

Can we blame someone else when we choose to sin, even if they deceived us?

No! We cannot blame anyone else and what’s more, GOD cannot forgive us unless we confess to what we have done wrong specifically and personally ask Him to forgive us. No one can do that on our behalf; certainly not a priest.

Can we simply say: “I’m sorry Jesus” and do that wrong thing again?

No. When we ask for forgiveness for something we have done wrong, we should try hard not to do it again. However, if we ask for forgiveness while already planning in our hearts to do it again, thinking that no one will know, then we are not sincerely sorry and God cannot forgive us.

Does God know what we think?

Oh yes because He is omniscient, which means, He knows everything! God always looks at what is in our hearts. We cannot hide anything from Him, not even our thoughts. Read 1 Sam 16:7.

GOD warned that they would die if they ate from the fruit of the forbidden tree. But why? Was the tree and its fruit poisonous?

No, there was nothing wrong with the tree or its fruit. Just like all the other things which God had created, the tree was ‘very good’. But God had forbidden Adam and Eve to eat of this specific tree as a test of their loyalty and obedience to Him.

Why does God have to punish sin?

Because God is 100% love, but also 100% righteous and holy, He must punish the sinner.

Did God still love Adam and Eve after they sinned?

Oh yes! Even though God was very sad about their wrong choice, He still loved them as much as before. God felt very sorry for them. It broke His heart to see their unhappiness. He was particularly sad because He knew what sad consequences sin would bring to them and also to all of their descendants right up to us today.

What ray of hope did God give to them?

One of the Persons of the Godhead, the Son of God and the Creator of heaven and earth, Jesus Christ, declared Himself willing to pay the penalty for sin on behalf of mankind — even before Creation occurred. This meant that He would sacrifice His Own life in the place of sinners such as Adam and Eve (and you and me).

Why did God make clothes of skin for them to wear?

God wanted to teach Adam and Eve about this Plan of Salvation and since God made clothes of skin, it meant that an innocent animal had to be slaughtered on their behalf; the first creature that ever died on earth. This was probably when they first learnt about sacrifice. In other words, because of sin, something had to die in their place as a type, or an example, of Christ, the true Sacrifice on their and our behalf.

Some people say that it was unfair and cruel of God to have his Son crucified, but is this true?

It is very important to understand that the Son Himself voluntarily offered to lay down His life because He loves the Father and us with infinite love! Nobody commanded the Son to do so and the Son did not make any decision on His own, but always in accordance with His Father’s will. The Plan of Salvation was therefore determined in unison by the Godhead. When JESUS says that He lay down His life Himself, it simply emphasizes that HE was not forced to do so, but did so voluntarily out of love for us and for the Father. Read John 10:17-18.

So what is this great hope that God had given to humanity here?

HE promised that hope and forgiveness would be available to all of humanity through JESUS CHRIST.
