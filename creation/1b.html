The Big Bang & Day 5

What is an atheist and how do they believe everything came into existence?

An atheist is someone who does not believe that God exists. They believe that everything came into existence, naturally from a big bang.

A short summary of the Big Bang and a few questions to think about Nothing exploded spontaneously to produce everything in existence today. Nothing - something — everything.

• No one was there, so how do they know if this really happened? • How could everything in existence today have been compressed into a single little dot? • What caused this single little dot to explode, in other words, where did all the energy needed for a so-called big bang to happen, come from? • How can an explosion cause everything to be perfectly organized afterwards? • How could the Earth have ‘landed’ in such a special position by chance with the exact and perfect conditions for people, plants and animals to live in (as explained in the previous episode)?

What does ‘ex nihilo’ mean?

It means ‘from nothing’. God created everything from nothing.

Is it possible that the universe (everything) came into existence by itself?

No, the universe could not have come into existence by itself, because the universe has not been existence forever. Only God exists forever, but the universe had a beginning; And as we learnt in the previous episode, everything that has a beginning, must have been made by someone. It had to have had a cause. If this were not so, the sun and the stars would have used up all of their energy and disappeared a long time ago. This is why we know that time, space, matter and energy (light) could not have made itself, because something that did not exist before cannot make anything

Does a Big Bang explosion make any sense at all?

No. The idea of a ‘big bang’ explosion to try and explain how everything originated and self- organized simply don’t make sense. Where did the energy needed for such an explosion come from and how can an explosion cause everything to be perfectly organized afterward? An explosion only causes utter chaos

Do you think that God needed a big bang or evolution to create everything?

No, because God is almighty. He did not need an explosion or evolution to create everything. HE only needs to speak, and it happens; and that is exactly what the Bible teaches. One needs A LOT of faith to be able to believe that everything in existence today originated spontaneously from nothing, without God.

How do we know that the Biblical Creation account is true?

Only GOD was there in the beginning and only GOD provided us with a trustworthy record of the Creation account. Through the Bible He provided Moses, (the author of amongst others, the book Genesis), with the correct sequence of the Creation events. Through faith, we choose to believe it.

What is a birth certificate and how does it work in the Bible

When you were born, your parents were given a birth certificate which contained the date and time that you were born. The Bible supplies us with a birth certificate for the universe.

What are genealogies and what do we learn from Biblical genealogies?

Genealogies are those lists and lists of names and ages found in the Bible. From these, we can see that the Creation is young — about 6,000 years old.

Can we trust God’s Word about the age of the Creation?

Yes. The most reliable way to determine the age of anything is through the testimony of a reliable witness — someone who was there. The Bible is the method of communication used by our Creator – by The One Who really was there! Since the Bible is God’s Word, we can know for sure that it is the only reliable source on the origin and age of the earth and the universe. It is the ’eye witness account’ of how God literally created the earth and the universe in six normal days.

But how did they calculate the estimated 6,000-year old age for the Creation?

Through this family history, an accurate record of past events is revealed to us dating from Genesis to Jesus’ first coming, and by adding the ages of our ancestors together, we can accurately calculate what happened when. So when you add all these ages together according to the genealogies, it is clear that Adam lived approximately 4,000 years before the coming of Christ and that the Biblical Flood took place around 2,500 years before Christ. By adding the last 2,000 years since Christ came to the 4,000 years before Christ came, we arrive at an age of approximately 6,000 years for the creation and therefore, the earth

What did God create on Day 5?

GOD created all the various water and flying creatures according to their kind.

Can you name a few water creatures?

Fish, whales, jellyfish, crabs, krill and corals, see stars, and plesiosaurs

Can you name a few flying creatures?

Birds, butterflies, bees, beetles, etc.

Our Bible mentions birds, but what about bats and flying reptiles (pterosaurs)?

The Hebrew word translated as ‘birds’ in our Bible is this word: ôph, and it means ‘flyers’ or ‘flying creatures’. Since bats, pterosaurs and insects obviously have wings and fly, grouping both them and birds together under the Hebrew label ‘winged flying creatures’ is completely accurate.

Are the wings of birds, bats, insects and flying reptiles the same?

No. Bird wings consist of feathers, but insect wings consist of membranes or sometimes of thin scales. Bats’ wings consist of skin stretched over the long arm and hand bones while the pterosaurs’ skin is stretched over the long fourth finger bone to form the wing.

How are birds adapted to fly?

Birds are specially designed to fly. Their bones are much lighter than our heavy, solid bones because birds’ bones are mostly hollow and full of air pockets. But their bones are not weak at all because they have special cross-members to support and strengthen the bones. Their bills are also designed to be very light and consists of keratin, the same material that is found in our hair and in a rhino’s horn. Birds also have very strong chest muscles and their lungs are specially designed so that they can absorb the maximum amount of oxygen.

How did mankind manage to get aircraft to fly?

By imitating birds. If it were not for the special design of birds, people would probably never have figured out how to design and make aircraft, since aircraft were built mainly by imitating the design of birds. Since it is quite clear that design (in other words, good planning and intelligence) was involved in building aircraft, we know that a very intelligent designer had to be involved in this process.

What do we learn from something that is designed?

Where there is design, there must always be a designer. Likewise today, when we look at all the living creatures as well as the non-living matter and we see how each organism is specially designed to adapt to its specific environment, then we know that our Almighty Creator GOD, is the Master Designer.

What does evolution teach?

Evolution teaches that all living organisms in existence today, originated (came into existence) naturally and spontaneously, by itself, without God. Evolutionists believe that all living organisms have changed or evolved from one kind into another kind over millions of years. This involves non-living matter (microbe) miraculously changing into some unknown small living creature consisting of only one cell, which then evolved over billions of years into the wide variety of living creatures that we can see today.

Some people believe that whales evolved from land animals. Why doesn’t this make sense?

Because there is no evidence for this and in anyway, GOD only created the land creatures on Day 6, after HE had created the sea creatures; So how could the sea creatures evolve from land animals if the land animals were not even in existence yet?
