Land & Vegetation

What did God create on Day 3?

Early on Day 3, our solid, spherical planet Earth was formed (shaped/given form)out of the ‘waters below’; so on Day three, God created the dry land mass and the plants. This land mass probably settled and separated from the water mass, (similar to when sediments in water settle) and then pushed up out of the water.

Were the plants mature/fully grown, or was it seed?

Each kind of plant was fully grown, in other words, it was mature and already contained its own specific seed within the plant so that it could bring forth more of its own kind. That is exactly how it still works today.
