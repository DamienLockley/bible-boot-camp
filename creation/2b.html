Destruction · Topic 2b

Consequences of the Fall

What changed after Eden

What is a prophecy?

A prophecy is a revelation from God to humanity about things that will happen in future; and because some of those things have already taken place, we can look back into history to see how perfectly God’s prophecies have been fulfilled.

Genesis 3 contains a very important prophecy. What is it about?

It explains how One of the descendants of the woman would one day bruise the serpent’s head and the serpent would bruise His heel.

What does it mean?

This prophecy refers to the fact that Jesus Christ would come to earth to completely destroy satan and his work of deception. Jesus would die on the cross on our behalf and because He rose again after three days, He conquered the curse of sin, which is death, once and for all — forever! And so Jesus bruised the serpent’s head and the serpent represents satan.

What did God tell Eve specifically?

That because of her sin, her pain and effort in child birth would be greatly increased.

What did God tell Adam specifically?

God told Adam that the earth was now cursed because of their sin and that it would yield thorns and thistles. These did not exist before the Fall. Where Adam’s work was an absolute pleasure to do in the past, it would now require a lot of effort. For the rest of their lives on earth, humanity would have to work very, very hard for the soil to yield their food.

Can we still see the consequences of the Fall in nature today?

Yes, we see it in a beautiful flower that wilts, falls to the ground, rots and dies. We see it in crops that fail, in droughts and in floods. We experience it when terrible storms and tsunamis devastate vast areas and we see it when we bite into a juicy mango just to find a worm inside that has already spoilt it. Read Rom 8:20-22

Why did God send Adam and Eve out of the Garden of Eden?

There was another tree in the Garden of Eden that God mentioned specifically; and that was the ‘Tree of Life’. In Rev 22:1-2 & 14 we are told that the fruit of this tree will bring healing and that everyone who goes to heaven will eat of this fruit and live with God, forever. So if Adam and Eve still had access to this tree in the Garden of Eden; and if they had eaten the fruit in their sinful state, mankind would have become immortal and would have had to live in this sinful condition forever. This is why God sent them out of the Garden and commanded an angel to guard the access to Eden and therefore also to the ‘Tree of Life’.

What else do we learn from this?

This proves once again, that mankind is mortal. We would only have been immortal if we had remained obedient and had unlimited access to the ‘Tree of Life’. Even though Adam eventually lived for 930 years, both Adam and Eve slowly began to die from that very moment when they chose to sin — just like us every day.

Why is there so much pain, suffering, disease, war and sadness in the world today? Is it

God’s fault? God created Adam and Eve to be perfect, healthy and happy and with the ability to choose between obedience or disobedience to God; just like we too have to make that choice every day. When we make a choice, we have to accept the consequences of that choice as well. When Adam and Eve chose disobedience, the consequences was that sin, disease, suffering and death came into the world. This was certainly not how God intended life for mankind to be. Read Rom 5:12.

What was the name of Adam and Eve’s first two sons and what did they do?

Cain was a crop farmer and Abel was a ‘keeper of sheep’.

Why did people in the Old Testament bring blood sacrifices to God?

A sacrifice to GOD had a very specific purpose. The innocent lamb was a symbol of Christ Who would sacrifice His innocent and sinless life to pay the penalty for the sins of fallen humanity. The blood of the lamb represented Christ’s blood that washes away our sin. This is why people in the Old Testament brought blood sacrifices to the Lord. In this way they showed that they understood how sin leads to death and that an innocent Lamb (Jesus Christ) would one day give the perfect sacrifice on behalf of all mankind. Read Heb 9:22.

Why did God reject Cain’s sacrifice, yet He accept that of Abel?

Because Cain sacrificed the yield of his crop, while Abel sacrificed his best lamb. So Abel brought a blood sacrifice, but Cain did not. Of course Cain could have traded his crop of fruit for one of Abel’s lambs, but he didn’t. The Bible further explains how Abel brought his sacrifice to God in faith because his heart was sincere, but Cain’s heart was not sincere & God knew this.

How did Cain react when God rejected his sacrifice?

Cain became very upset and angry

How did God try and help Cain to turn away from sin and did Cain listen?

Read Gen 4:6-7. No, he did not listen, he ignored it and consequently, he just became angrier and angrier.

What did all this pent-up anger of Cain eventually lead to?

One day, when Cain and Abel were alone in the field, Cain’s pent-up anger suddenly boiled over and the next moment he struck Abel, his own brother, and killed him! This was the very first murder ever on earth.

What very important lesson do we learn from this?

We learn that we should not give free reign to our emotions — it can destroy us and those around us. We have to pray daily and ask God to help us to remain in control of our emotions. Our emotions (especially anger) should not control us, but rather, we should remain in control of our emotions. Fact is, when we are angry, we cannot think clearly and we sometimes do very dumb things…

Do we still have a sacrificial system today where we need to bring sacrifices?

No, the entire system came to an end after Jesus, the perfect Lamb of God, brought the perfect sacrifice. That single and perfect sacrifice that replaced the old testament blood sacrifices, was the crucifixion of Jesus Christ. This is why God no longer wants us to bring any other blood- sacrifices. Read Heb 10:10.

What was the name of the first son that Adam and Eve had after Abel’s death and what does his name mean?

Seth and that means ‘substitute’

Who was Cain’s wife?

In Genesis 5:4 we learn that Adam and Eve had other sons...ssss and other daughters...sss. Adam lived for a whopping 930 years. That would have been ample time to build quite a substantial nation. Since Adam and Eve were the first and only people on earth and since they had many children, Cain and Seth and their other brothers must have married their own sisters!

What are mutations?

Your body is composed of billions of tiny little cells, and inside each tiny little cell, we have even tinier things called ‘genes’. These genes carry all the information that we inherited from our parents and which we will one day carry over to our own children. As these genes are carried over from one generation to the next, mistakes sometimes slip in. We call these mistakes, ‘mutations’ and mutations are responsible for hundreds of hereditary diseases such as cancer and various birth defects. Over the years, these mutations have increased in each new generation. Different families have different mutations. So two people who come from different families, usually carry different mutations (mutant genes) and when they get married and have children, the ‘good’ genes (or the genes that are not mutated) will be ‘in charge’ in their children. So their children should not have any defects or diseases caused by mutations. However, when a brother and sister get married and have children, the chances of defects and mutation diseases in their children are much higher because a brother and a sister come from the same family and therefore have a greater chance of bearing the same harmful mutations in their genes.

Isn’t it wrong for a brother to marry his sister?

In the beginning, mutations was not a problem. They didn’t even exist because Adam and Eve were created perfect. But because of the Fall, everything was affected negatively — even those tiny particles that we call ‘genes’. Initially, the appearance of genetic mutations were still very low and that is why there were no genetic problems when Adam and Eve’s children married one other and had children. It took many hundreds of years for mutations to increase to such an extent that it became dangerous for a brother to marry his sister. This is why, in the time of Moses (about 2,500 years later), GOD gave specific instructions on this. Read this in Lev 18:9 and Deut 27:22. This explains why it was acceptable for brothers and sisters to marry one another in the beginning, but not anymore today.

What about cousins — can they get married and have children?

When first cousins marry and have children, the chances of them carrying a disease forming mutation to their children, is one in eight! That is a very high chance!
