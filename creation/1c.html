Creation Week · Topic 1c

Day 6a — Land Animals

Life upon the land

What did God create first on Day 6?

All the land animals

Can you name a few land animals?

Wolves, elephants, cats, crocodiles, squirrels, rabbits, apes, mice, giraffes, etc.

When did God create dinosaurs and why do you say that?

Also on Day 6 because dinosaurs were land animals (land reptiles) and God said that He created all the land animals on Day 6

Why don’t we see the word ‘dinosaur’ in the Bible?

Because the Bible-translators did not yet know the word ‘dinosaur’. They did the translations in the 1500’s and 1600’s, but Sir Richard Owens only invented the word ‘dinosaur’ in the 1800’s. That’s almost 400 years after the first Bible translations were done.

What word did the translators possibly use for dinosaurs?

‘Dragon’ or ‘monster’ in the new translations

What does the word ‘dinosaur’ mean?

It means ‘terrible lizard’

Were dinosaurs lizards?

No, but when scientists first saw their bones, they thought that it looked a lot like huge lizard bones. Dinosaurs were unique land reptiles

How do dinosaurs differ from lizards?

The most obvious difference is that dinosaurs walk with ‘upright’ legs, rather than the ‘sideways’ legs of typical reptiles including some lizards.

School text books state and most of the world believe that dinosaurs went extinct 65 million years ago. Yet the Bible indicates that the lived around 6,000 years ago. How do we make sense of this?

The Bible teaches that GOD made ALL the land animals, on the sixth day and since dinosaurs were land animals, this must have included them. Dinosaurs could not have gone extinct 65 million years ago, because dinosaurs and death did not even exist. The Bible teaches that death came into the world because of Adam and Eve’s sin. It is also called, the ‘Fall’ and the Fall only took place approximately 6,000 years ago. So, how could anything have died before that time, in other words, 65 million years ago, if the earth is only about 6,000 years old and sin and death did not exist yet?

Are there other references to dragons, except in the Bible, which shows that humans possibly lived with them and knew what they looked like?

Yes, worldwide there are historic references to these dragons. King George fought a dragon which is today identified as a dinosaur called a Nothosaurus. Alexander the Great’s army were scared of by a dinosaur in a cave, while Marco Polo reported seeing a dinosaur while visiting China. Chinese Historians show dinosaurs along with other known animals in the Chinese Zodiac. We also have cave drawings, tapestries and carvings of dinosaurs showing that people knew what they looked like (even before their fossils were discovered), since they were alive at the same time!

In Job 40 God is probably describing a dinosaur that He created. What was it?

The Behemoth is described in our Bibles as either an elephant, because it is the largest land animal today, or a hippopotamus, because they sometimes live in marshy areas.

Is it true that the elephant is the largest land animal God ever created?

No, the fossil record (earth’s cemetery of the Flood) clearly confirms that the elephant is definitely not the largest land animal that GOD made. Elephants are dwarfed by the biggest dinosaurs!

Why couldn’t the animal in Job 40 have been an elephant or a hippopotamus?

Because the tail of this animal is compared to a cedar tree, known in the Bible for its size and strength, which is why it is a very strong indication that this animal was absolutely enormous in size. The hippo only has a short little stump, while the elephant has a thin, cordlike tail. Behemoth’s strength was in his hips, while that of the elephant is in the neck, head and trunk; Furthermore Behemoth’s legs and rib bones were solid bone, while those of the hippo and elephant have marrow cavities.

Which animal is possibly being described here?

It was probably a Brachiosaurus - a sauropod type dinosaur; and they are in all probability, the largest land animals that God ever created.

The Bible speaks of the fruit of the spirit. Do you know what that is?

These are the good fruit that we, as Christians should bear as an example to other people so that they can see that we are GOD’S children. These fruit are: ‘Love, joy peace, long-suffering (patience), kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control’ (Gal 5:22)

Which animal is being described in Job 41?

Leviathan was probably a Sarchosucchus imperator (Supercroc)

Do you still think the lion is the king of beasts?

No, because God said that Leviathan was the king of beasts

Why did God describe these two impressive animals in such detail?

Because God wanted to reminded Job and us as well, about all the wonderful aspects of His marvelous creation. God is pointing Job to the magnificence of His Creation by firstly pointing out the largest and most magnificent animal that ever lived on earth (‘behemoth’) and then, HE describes the most fearsome ‘king of beasts’, the ‘leviathan’ in Job 41.’Leviathan’ is also a symbol for satan. By doing so, God teaches us that only HE, Who is GOD, can overcome and achieve the victory over satan, who is also a created being. We are reminded that GOD still remains in control of our lives, even when satan attempts to destroy us.
