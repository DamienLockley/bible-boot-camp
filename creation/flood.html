The earth’s surface consists of a mosaic of rigid plates, each moving as the other moves. This movement or drifting apart of the continents is called continental drift.

What is a fossil?

It is the remains of a once living, ancient organism that is embedded and preserved in rock. It includes plants, animals and their parts (shells, teeth, bones, or complete skeletons), as well as the tracks that they left. Some fossils no longer contain the organism’s original material, but consist of minerals that penetrated the organism to take on its form. These minerals eventually hardened to stone (this is called petrification) and so the minerals are in the exact shape of the organism, or a part of it, but the organism itself is missing.
