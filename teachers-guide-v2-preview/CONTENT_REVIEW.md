# Teachers’ Guide V2 — Content and Teaching Structure Review

**Draft only; no change to the live website, original Word master, or original study data.**

This review map preserves the original 28 studies and their teaching items in their existing order. The new navigation groups the material into a core presentation and optional question-indexed references. The teacher selects any study without a mandatory sequence. All new application/response prompts and the two missing introductions are proposals, not alterations to the author's original Word source.

## Key content decisions reflected in the draft

1. **Sanctuary:** the existing appeal ends the core pathway; the sanctuary furniture, deeper typology, and final apologetics remain available as optional reference, never discarded.
2. **Antichrist:** the concise opening pathway keeps Jesus central. The longer summary, historical identifications, claims, sources, and Bible-marking notes are grouped by question for teachers to find when needed. Historical assertions are not treated as independently fact-checked.
3. **The Mark of the Beast / America in Bible Prophecy:** lengthy historical quotations are grouped into topical questions, not required stops during the core presentation.
4. **The Sabbath, Hell, and similar studies:** difficult texts are accessible as named questions, with complete original context preserved.
5. **Historicity of Jesus:** the salvation/Calvinism tail is preserved but provisionally classified as supplementary, pending a decision about whether it belongs in that study.
6. **1844:** the original “Part two: (1844 controversy)?” material is provisionally supplementary, subject to review.
7. **Every study:** a short spiritual application and an invitation or reflection question are proposed. Teachers can skip or adapt them; they are not compulsory scripts.

## Content that needs explicit approval before merging into a Word master

- **New introductions** for *Historicity of Jesus* and *The Blue Stone* (their current source material lacks Purpose / Centred on statements).
- **The exact question labels and group boundaries**, particularly in the lengthy Antichrist appendix, to ensure they are neutral and helpful.
- **The proposed spiritual application and invitation** for each study.
- **The supplementary placements** noted above. Nothing has been moved or removed from the Word source.
- **Historical quotation checking** (dates, attribution, quotations and interpretation) is a separate research task if requested; none has been silently corrected.

## All 28 study drafts

### 1. The Word of God

**Purpose:** To show the Bible is trustworthy

**Central truth:** Claims ,Content & Consistency; God wants to commune with us

**Core teaching stages:** Scripture's claims → God's character revealed → Predictive prophecy and Daniel 2 → Consistency of Scripture → Invitation to continue.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Scripture is an invitation to know God's character, not merely collect information.

**Proposed response / invitation:** Would you like to keep discovering what Scripture reveals about God?

### 2. The Great Controversy

**Purpose:** To show there is a cosmic conflict between God and Satan, that planet Earth is its theatre and worship is its centre

**Central truth:** Origin of evil, Outworking of evil & Obsolescence (end) of evil.

**Core teaching stages:** Where did evil begin? → The conflict over worship → God's answer and the end of evil.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Why does God permit evil?
- Why create Lucifer knowing rebellion was possible?
- What about free will and suffering?
- Could God have created beings without freedom?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Contrast God's love and compassion with coercion and rebellion.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What does the contrast between God's way and Satan's way show you about His character?

### 3. The Work of the Holy Spirit

**Purpose:** To show how the spirit works on you & in you to bring about conversion.

**Central truth:** The work of the Holy Spirit

**Core teaching stages:** The Spirit convicts and guides → The Spirit and God's Word → New life in Christ → The Spirit changes the heart.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Transformation involves the Spirit's work, not mere agreement with a doctrine.

**Proposed response / invitation:** Where might you invite the Holy Spirit to lead you?

### 4. The Sanctuary

**Purpose:** To tell the story of redemption and reveal Gods plan to separate sinners from sin. (God is constant / Man is variable)

**Central truth:** Jesus. How God deals with the separation problem.

**Core teaching stages:** The separation caused by sin → God seeks to dwell with us → The sanctuary points to Jesus → Personal invitation and response.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What does the furniture represent?
- How do the furnishings point to Christ?
- What do the rooms and furnishings teach?
- How do the stages of salvation relate?
- Can salvation be rejected?

**Proposed spiritual application:** God has moved toward us to restore the relationship damaged by sin.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What does God's desire to live close to you mean personally?

### 5. The Plan of Salvation

**Purpose:** To show that salvation is by Grace alone through faith alone to the Glory of God alone.

**Central truth:** The problem and the solution.

**Core teaching stages:** Sin and our need → God's love revealed at the cross → What Jesus accomplished → Receiving the gift → An appropriate invitation.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Grace is received, not earned; the cross reveals God's love.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How might you respond to God's gift of grace?

### 6. The Law

**Purpose:** To demonstrate from scripture that God’s law is all about love and liberty, that is to be internally written rather than externally imposed and it is eternal.

**Central truth:** The Decalogue.

**Core teaching stages:** The law of love and liberty → Grace produces obedience from the heart.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Is the law still relevant under the new covenant?
- Does Christ as the end of the law abolish it?
- What does it mean to die to the law?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Obedience is the fruit of salvation, not its price.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How would following Jesus from love differ from trying to earn His favour?

### 7. Antichrist

**Purpose:** To identify the person and principle of antichrist.

**Central truth:** Jesus is Christ and anyone who turns people from Jesus or attempts to occupy the place of Jesus is putting themselves in the place of Christ and is antichrist. Jesus only is our guide and anything and everything that scatters from him is antichrist.

**Core teaching stages:** The meaning and spirit of antichrist → Daniel 7 and identifying characteristics → Keeping Jesus at the centre.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Does anti mean against or in place of?
- What are the issues of worship and obedience?
- How are the beasts and prophetic symbols interpreted?
- What historical identifying characteristics are discussed?
- Who were the ten horns and divided kingdoms?
- What authority claims are cited?
- What sources discuss persecution and prophetic time?
- What claims about papal authority are quoted?
- What is said about forgiveness of sins?
- How can the teacher mark Bible references?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Keep loyalty to Jesus central, without directing hostility toward individual believers.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What would keeping Christ at the centre of your faith look like?

### 8. The Seventy Week Prophecy

**Purpose:** To reveal the absolutely compelling evidence that Jesus Christ is the Messiah! To draw attention to an ancient prophecy that’s gives the key to unlock the most significant event this world has ever witnessed.

**Central truth:** Daniel 9.

**Core teaching stages:** The prophecy and its purpose → The Messiah and prophetic milestones → Fulfilment and gospel proclamation.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What other evidence and details are given?
- How does Stephen's ministry connect?
- How do Daniel 8 and 9 connect?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Prophecy is intended to strengthen confidence in Jesus as Messiah.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How do these Bible connections affect your confidence in Christ?

### 9. Historicity of Jesus

**Purpose (proposed):** To examine non-Christian historical references to Jesus and the evidence for His ministry and the early Christian movement.

**Central truth (proposed):** Historical sources and evidence surrounding the person of Jesus.

**Core teaching stages:** Non-Christian Greco-Roman sources → Ancient Jewish sources → What these sources say about Jesus.

**Optional reference questions:**

- How does this relate to holiness and salvation?
- Five points of Calvinism (placement for review)

**Proposed spiritual application:** Examine historical evidence carefully without fearing honest questions.

**Proposed response / invitation:** Would you like to examine the Gospels in the light of these historical sources?

### 10. The Judgement

**Purpose:** The Judgement is to vindicate the Character of God.

**Central truth:** In the Judgement, God is really on trial and not just us.

**Core teaching stages:** Why judgement reveals God's character → The stages and participants of judgement → The standard and Christ's role.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Consider the fairness and openness of God's judgement alongside Jesus' saving work.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What does God's commitment to justice mean to you?

### 11. 1844

**Purpose:** To show that the judgement is the culmination of the cross and that the final phase of Christs ministry in the heavenly sanctuary began in 1844.

**Central truth:** The climax of the Gospel.

**Core teaching stages:** Sanctuary and Day of Atonement → Christ's heavenly ministry → The gospel significance.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Why is 1844 debated?
- How do Daniel's visions relate to the chronology?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Jesus' ministry is presented as an expression of God's commitment to salvation and justice.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How does Christ's continuing ministry encourage your trust?

### 12. America in Bible Prophecy

**Purpose:** To identify the second beast of Revelation 13.

**Central truth:** The identification characteristics.

**Core teaching stages:** The second beast → Religion, government and freedom.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What quotations concern religion and government?
- What is said about church and state?
- What is the historical Holy Office discussion?
- What is quoted about Sunday?
- What other political quotations are included?

**Proposed spiritual application:** True worship involves freedom of conscience rather than coercion.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How can we respect conscience while remaining faithful to our convictions?

### 13. The Blue Stone:

**Purpose (proposed):** To explore the links presented between sanctuary imagery, prophecy and God's law, leading to our need of Jesus.

**Central truth (proposed):** The law reveals God's righteousness and our need of a Saviour; obedience responds to grace.

**Core teaching stages:** Prophetic symbols in Revelation 17 → Sanctuary colours and imagery → God's law leads us to Jesus.

**Proposed spiritual application:** God's law reveals our need of Christ; it does not replace His grace.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How does the law point you toward Jesus rather than self-reliance?

### 14. The Sabbath

**Purpose:** To demonstrate from scripture that the Sabbath is a memorial of both salvation & creation.

**Central truth:** Jesus came to our world as the creator to re-create humanity in God’s image. By his life and death he finished the work of salvation and rested in the tomb on the Sabbath.

**Core teaching stages:** Sabbath and Creation → Creation, re-creation and salvation → Worship and seventh-day rest.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What early Christian sources discuss Sunday?
- What does Acts 20:7 mean?
- How are the difficult passages interpreted?
- What about Colossians and other texts?
- What does the historical appendix say?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Sabbath rest points to our Creator and Redeemer, not earned salvation.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How might time with God deepen your relationship with Him?

### 15. The Second Coming

**Purpose:** To show that the second coming of Christ is the pinnacle of salvation history and that it is literal, visible, audible and POWERFUL.

**Central truth:** Jesus is coming to restore to deliver and to make an end of sin, pain and death.

**Core teaching stages:** The promise of Christ's return → What happens at His coming?.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Will the coming be literal, visible and audible?
- What about coming as a thief?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Christ's return offers hope that suffering and evil will end.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How does the promise of Jesus' return affect your hope?

### 16. The Nature of Man

**Purpose:** To show that man does not possess immortality, life is found in Christ alone. To identify man’s soul and what happens when we die.

**Central truth:** Jesus holds the keys of hell and death that unlocks the mystery of these important subjects. Jesus’ death loses its mystery and its sting and becomes a blessed rest on the journey home.

**Core teaching stages:** What is a living soul? → How Scripture describes death → Hope in the resurrection.

**Optional reference questions:**

- How should difficult passages about death be understood?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Hope in life after death rests in Christ and His resurrection promise.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How does the resurrection promise speak to your experience of loss?

### 17. The Mark of the Beast

**Purpose:** To identify the Beast and its Mark.

**Central truth:** Jesus alone is worthy of our Worship and Allegiance, for he is our God, Creator and Redeemer.

**Core teaching stages:** Worship and the mark → An appeal to follow Christ.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What does the symbolism mean?
- What sources discuss the Sabbath and Sunday?
- What denominational statements are quoted?
- What other historical sources are cited?
- What claims are made about enforcement?
- What else does the historical appendix contain?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Allegiance to Jesus matters more than condemnation of other people.

**Proposed response / invitation:** Whose authority do you trust when your convictions are challenged?

### 18. The Millennium

**Purpose:** To explain millennium and purpose – What happens during and after it.

**Central truth:** The Goodness of God, seen at the cross is demonstrated in the Millennium Judgement.

**Core teaching stages:** Why a millennium? → Coming and resurrection → The thousand years → The end of the conflict.

**Proposed spiritual application:** God's final resolution of evil is presented as fair and transparent.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What picture of God's fairness emerges from this study?

### 19. Hell – Is God a Sadist?

**Purpose:** To show that the purpose of Hell is to destroy sin forever.

**Central truth:** God’s righteousness.

**Core teaching stages:** What does Scripture say about hell? → When and how judgement occurs → God's character and the end of sin → An appeal grounded in mercy.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What about the rich man and Lazarus?
- Does forever always mean endless duration?
- What about the worm?
- Are there spirits in hell?
- What does the great gulf mean?

**Proposed spiritual application:** God's judgement should be considered alongside His desire to end suffering.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What picture of God's character emerges from these passages?

### 20. The Gift of Prophecy

**Purpose:** To show how God does everything in his power to guide us thru the gift of prophecy.

**Central truth:** Jesus Christ gives the right message at the right time.

**Core teaching stages:** Why God sends prophets → A pattern of prophetic guidance → Testing prophetic claims → Responding to God's guidance.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Can women be prophets?
- How should prophetic writings be tested?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Prophetic guidance must deepen trust in Jesus and be tested against Scripture.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How can you be open to guidance while remaining grounded in the Bible?

### 21. The Remnant Church

**Purpose:** To Discover which Church is theremnant church of the last days.

**Central truth:** Since JESUS gives his life his sheep follow him wherever he goes.

**Core teaching stages:** Christ gathers His flock → Symbols and God's people → Identifying characteristics → Responding to Christ.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What difficult texts concern the church?
- What historical events relate to 1798?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Belonging to God's people means following Jesus and treating others with love.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What does it mean to follow the Shepherd wherever He leads?

### 22. Christian living

**Purpose:** To explore the topic of adornment and to find out how much is too much.

**Central truth:** Jewellery and other associated material.

**Core teaching stages:** Identity and inner change → Adornment and priorities → Humility and witness → Christ our example.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Christian choices should flow from a changed heart rather than mere appearance.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What priorities might Christ be inviting you to reconsider?

### 23. Prayer

**Purpose:** Prayer is powerful, effectual, and necessary for the disciple of Christ. There are certain conditions that will ensure that our prayers are heard.

**Central truth:** Prayer is how we communicate with our saviour.

**Core teaching stages:** Jesus' example of prayer → Faith and God's will → A life of prayer → An invitation to pray.

**Optional reference questions:**

- Does posture matter in prayer?
- What about faith and unanswered prayer?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Prayer is trustful relationship with God, not a method of controlling outcomes.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What would you like to bring to God in prayer?

### 24. Health

**Purpose:** To show our bodies are the temple of the Holy Spirit. As our creator, redeemer and father, God is intimately concerned with the health of the whole person-body, mind & spirit.

**Central truth:** Jesus Christ came so that we may have life & life more abundantly.

**Core teaching stages:** Health and the whole person → God's original plan → Nutrition and care → Temperance.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What about ritual cleanliness?
- What passages discuss wine?
- What about Paul's advice and dietary liberty?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Caring for health expresses gratitude and stewardship rather than human worth.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What practical step toward healthier living would you consider?

### 25. Baptism

**Purpose:** To show the ritual/ceremony of Baptism is a symbol of the turning away from sin and a turning to the Lord.

**Central truth:** Baptism by immersion.

**Core teaching stages:** The example and meaning of baptism → Repentance and discipleship → Invitation to follow Christ.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What about baptism objections?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Baptism represents turning from sin and beginning a life of discipleship.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What further step of following Jesus might you take?

### 26. The Godhead

**Purpose:** To show the Father, Son and the Holy Spirit are a unity of 3 co-eternal persons.

**Central truth:** Jesus, Love and unity

**Core teaching stages:** The Father and the Son → The Spirit and the unity of the Godhead.

**Optional reference questions:**

- What does Scripture say about Christ's divinity?

**Proposed spiritual application:** Knowledge about the Godhead should deepen love, worship and trust.

**Proposed response / invitation:** What have you discovered about the Father, Son and Holy Spirit?

### 27. Stewardship

**Purpose:** Everything belongs to God; as stewards we are responsible to Him for all that He has entrusted to us.

**Central truth:** Jesus Christ gave all for us and calls us to be fully surrendered to Him.

**Core teaching stages:** Everything belongs to God → Stewardship and faithful giving → Following Jesus' example.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Stewardship reflects gratitude for what God has entrusted to us.

**Proposed response / invitation:** How might God's generosity change your use of resources and time?

### 28. Witnessing

**Purpose:** To show that every true disciple of Christ is born into the kingdom of God as a missionary.

**Central truth:** Jesus.

**Core teaching stages:** Called to witness → Sharing what Christ has done → A personal invitation to serve.

**Proposed spiritual application:** Witnessing flows from personally knowing and following Jesus.

**Proposed response / invitation:** Who might benefit from hearing what Jesus has done in your life?
